Abhiram Maddukuri

I am a Masters computer science student at UT Austin, fortunate to be advised by Professor Yuke Zhu and Georgios Pavlakos.

I am broadly interested in harnessing diverse data sources to build generalist agents. My past work has focused on developing large-scale simulation platforms and exploring how to effectively leverage simulation datasets for imitation learning.
